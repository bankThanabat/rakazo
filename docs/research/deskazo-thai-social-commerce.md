# Deskazo social channels and commerce in Thailand and Asia

Research date: 2026-09-18. This is a provider shortlist and API feasibility review, not a promise of working integrations. No accounts or live APIs were tested. OpenConnector coverage is inventoried separately; inclusion here does not mean a provider is missing from OpenConnector.

## Recommendation

For Thailand, prioritize LINE Official Account and Facebook Messenger for customer conversations, then Instagram for merchants whose sales already originate there. Prioritize Shopee, TikTok Shop, and Lazada for marketplace product and order context. This is a product recommendation based on the evidence below, not a claim that all six belong in the first release.

Follow the requested sequence: first ship complete workflows using suitable existing OpenConnector capabilities; then extend the connector layer for the highest-value missing capabilities. A provider entry alone is insufficient. Customer messaging needs incoming events, replies, account routing, history where available, and human takeover. Catalog or advertising actions do not establish messaging support.

Shopify and WooCommerce are sensible early store connections if OpenConnector already supports the needed actions. Their technical fit does not establish that they are Thailand's most-used storefronts. WhatsApp is a separate Southeast Asian expansion candidate, especially for Indonesia. Japan and Taiwan make LINE reuse attractive. Mainland China requires a separate Weixin assessment.

## OpenConnector capability cross-check

The companion [action inventory](deskazo-openconnector-catalog.tsv), at upstream revision `20f86718e7bb938c2d4380c8bbb335cbaebb94bc`, changes what can ship first:

- LINE has five profile/push/broadcast actions, without incoming-message ingestion, reply-token sends, or history. Instagram has nine owned-media/comment and send-message actions, without DM history. WhatsApp has seventeen messaging/template actions, without history.
- `meta` has four identity/advertising actions, without native Pages/Messenger operations. TikTok Business has fifteen advertising/GMV Max actions; these do not expose Shop orders or customer-service conversations. Opaque MCP entry points do not prove those capabilities.
- Shopify Admin and WooCommerce each have twenty-six actions for store operations. Legacy `shopify` has thirteen blog/page actions and must not be mistaken for Shopify Admin.
- No dedicated native merchant connector was found for Shopee, Lazada, WeChat, or LINE SHOPPING/MyShop. This does not mean the brand names never appear. Sorftime offers Shopee product research; LinkFox/UnifAPI offer TikTok research; RedFox offers WeChat public-article research. These do not grant merchant order, inventory, or inbox access.
- Unipile has six read-only chat/message actions including a Messenger account schema. Ayrshare's eleven actions cover posting/history/analytics, and respond.io's thirteen cover contacts/assignment. None establishes a complete reply workflow in this inventory.

Therefore the existing catalog supports useful store-data and outbound-action prototypes, but does not by itself establish Deskazo's full Thai incoming-chat workflow. Missing actions on an existing provider should be extended before inventing a parallel provider integration.

## Consequences for the Deskazo spec

These are proposed requirements inferred from the API constraints and the product vision:

1. Describe brand-voice onboarding as learning from connected content and available conversation history. If a channel cannot provide history, accept merchant-approved examples or uploaded exports, then learn from new conversations and human steering.
2. Store learned voice as editable knowledge documents with source references and Space defaults plus bot overrides. Keep live inventory, promotions, and order status in connected systems; a voice document must not become the stock ledger.
3. Do not promise that connecting a store also connects its chat, social posts, or comments. Each needs a verified capability and the correct authorization.
4. Human alerts and takeover must work when replies are blocked by a messaging window, missing permission, expired authorization, exhausted quota, or provider failure.
5. Measure completion by a merchant journey: incoming question, verified SKU/stock answer, steer or handoff, agreed sale, and an authorized downstream action. App-count totals do not prove this workflow.

## Evidence gaps before choosing the final release set

- Cross-check each exact action and event against the OpenConnector inventory. Provider presence and official API availability are separate facts.
- Obtain current Shopee developer documentation and merchant/partner eligibility for seller chat.
- Verify TikTok Business Messaging and TikTok Shop customer-service entitlements independently with Thai developer accounts before committing release dates.
- Verify Facebook public-comment reply and owned-content ingestion scopes, review requirements, and pagination separately from Messenger.
- Establish real history depth and retention for Meta, TikTok Shop, Lazada, and WhatsApp onboarding. This research confirms that some history APIs exist, not that a complete merchant archive is available.
- Gather pilot merchants' channel mix and order volume. Audience figures, older multi-select surveys, and GMV estimates measure different things.
- For Malaysia, Singapore, Vietnam, and the Philippines, gather current country-specific messaging adoption before claiming a regional leader. This report does not equate WhatsApp's global scale with uniform Asian usage.
- CRM and invoicing providers, Thai tax-document requirements, and the full five-category OpenConnector catalog are separate companion research tasks.
