# Deskazo business integrations for Thailand and Southeast Asia

Researched 18 September 2026. Scope: CRM, invoicing, stock and order management, documents, and analytics. Research only; no provider accounts or live integrations were tested. OpenConnector availability is assessed separately in the integration inventory. Recommendations below concern product fit and provider feasibility, not proof that a connector completes the workflow.

## What adoption evidence actually supports

The best comparative evidence found is Content Shifu and Hummingbirds Consulting's original Thai MarTech survey. Its 2025 awards used over 500 respondents across industries, with SMEs defined as organizations of 1–100 people. SME winners included HubSpot for B2B CRM, ChocoCRM for B2C CRM, Page365 for OMS, Google Workspace for business operations, Google Analytics for analytics, and Looker Studio for dashboards. Readyplanet won local B2B CRM; Salesforce, Buzzebees and Microsoft 365 won their respective larger-business categories. These are survey results, not a census of Thai e-commerce businesses or market share by revenue. [Original awards and methodology, 14 February 2025](https://contentshifu.com/news/thailands-martech-awards-2025/)

The 2026 survey sampled over 530 Thai MarTech users. Its popularity awards again named HubSpot for B2B CRM, ChocoCRM for B2C CRM, Google Analytics, Looker Studio and Google Workspace in their categories. It does not establish each product's share among Deskazo's exact target customers. [Original 2026 results, 9 March 2026](https://contentshifu.com/news/thailands-martech-report-and-awards-2026/)

No comparable primary-source Thai SME market-share ranking was established for accounting providers. First-party customer totals below are useful demand signals but use different units and cannot rank providers against each other. For Asia expansion, choose countries explicitly. Thai accounting, Indonesian accounting and Singapore invoicing are separate integration decisions.

## Recommended order

1. Use the existing connector catalog first for Google Sheets/Drive, HubSpot, and a generic invoice workflow with a supported invoice provider. Sheets can cover a merchant's existing sales log without forcing a CRM migration. Keep inventory reads separate from stock reservation and order writes.
2. For Thai extensions, prioritize FlowAccount for invoicing and ZORT for stock/orders, then PEAK according to pilot customers' existing accounting software. Begin API discovery for Page365 and ChocoCRM early because their adoption evidence is stronger than their publicly established third-party API access.
3. Add Readyplanet or Zoho for merchants already using them. Add Excel/OneDrive when an actual Microsoft 365 merchant needs them. Analytics and loyalty integrations follow the reply-to-sale workflow.
4. Consider Mekari Jurnal for an Indonesian expansion. Do not present it, Xero, or Zoho Books as a verified Thai tax-compliance solution.

The sequence is a product recommendation. OpenConnector-first should not cause Deskazo to advertise Thai tax invoices before a suitable provider and document flow have been verified.

### Invoice is not the same as Thai e-tax invoice

Deskazo should distinguish a quotation, payment-request invoice, receipt, Thai tax invoice, and electronic tax invoice. An API returning a PDF or an object called `invoice` is insufficient evidence of Thai e-tax compliance. Thailand's official system has its own preparation/submission processes, including service-provider arrangements; ETDA also validates electronic signatures and timestamps for the different e-tax document schemes. [Revenue Department overview](https://etax.rd.go.th/etax_staticpage/app/emag/flipbook_2567/pdf/01_Overview.pdf), [ETDA document validation](https://www.etda.or.th/en/Our-Service/Digital-Trusted-services-Infrastructure/TEDA/Web-Validation.aspx)

For the spec, let the merchant select an authorized provider and document type. Validate business/customer identity, line items, configured taxes, issue status, document number and delivery status using that provider. Record the external document ID so a retry does not issue a second invoice. The exact fiscal requirements remain a provider acceptance task; this research does not certify a generated document.

## Acceptance questions before promising an integration

- Can a normal merchant connect and revoke access without a developer or provider employee?
- Can Deskazo read the actual source of truth for SKU, variant, availability, promotion dates and customer history?
- Are writes supported for the exact action, with an external ID and a safe retry strategy?
- Can the merchant review an invoice or CRM update before it is committed when approval is configured?
- Does the provider permit the intended storage and AI processing? Brand-voice learning should update merchant-controlled knowledge; it must not silently train models on restricted provider data.
- What happens after expired credentials, revoked access, a changed spreadsheet column, stale stock, insufficient API credits or a provider outage?

Remaining evidence gaps are current accounting/OMS market share, third-party access contracts for Page365/ChocoCRM/Shipnity/Sellsuki, and live merchant-account verification. None should be replaced with a claim that a logo in a connector catalog proves support.
